# English for AI and Online Work Resources

## Knowledge

- [OpenAI: Prompt engineering](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/prompt-engineering)
  Use for: writing clearer AI requests, specifying goals, context, constraints, and validation steps.
- [OpenAI Help: Best practices for prompt engineering](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6654000-best-practices-for-prompt-engineering-with-the-openai-api)
  Use for: simple prompt patterns and practical reminders when asking AI to do work.
- [Google for Developers: Technical Writing](https://developers.google.com/tech-writing)
  Use for: writing clear instructions, documentation, and work notes.
- [Digital.gov: Plain language guide series](https://digital.gov/guides/plain-language)
  Use for: making English easier to read, understand, and act on.
- [British Council LearnEnglish: English for emails](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/free-resources/business/english-emails)
  Use for: workplace email structure, useful phrases, and practice activities.
- [Purdue OWL: Email Etiquette](https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/academic_writing/email_etiquette.html)
  Use for: professional email tone, subject lines, short paragraphs, and direct requests.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [OpenAI Developer Community](https://community.openai.com/)
  Use for: seeing how real users ask questions about AI tools, prompts, and workflows.
- [Google Developer Community](https://developers.google.com/community)
  Use for: broader technical communication and online work practices.
