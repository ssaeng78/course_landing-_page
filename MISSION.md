# Mission: English for AI and Online Work

## Why
The user wants practical English that helps them work faster online: asking AI for better help, reading tool documentation, writing clear messages, and managing content or web projects with less friction. The goal is not general school English, but usable English for real tasks.

## Success looks like
- Write clear English prompts that tell AI the goal, context, constraints, and desired output.
- Read short documentation pages and extract the action needed.
- Write simple professional emails or messages for online work.
- Check and improve English text with AI without losing the intended meaning.
- Build a small personal phrase bank for repeated work situations.

## Constraints
- Lessons should be short, practical, and usable within 15-30 minutes.
- Thai explanations are welcome, but practice should include English output.
- Focus on online work, AI tools, content work, websites, and automation workflows.
- Every lesson should include retrieval practice, not just reading.

## Out of scope
- Exam preparation such as TOEIC, IELTS, or academic grammar drills.
- Advanced literary writing.
- Long theory lessons without immediate work use.
